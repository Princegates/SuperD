The fare

Worked out the same way every time

A base fare, plus a rate for every kilometre the rider actually rides. Both are set per zone, and the customer sees the total before they confirm.

Platform defaults. A vendor’s zone can carry its own base fare and per-kilometre rate — whatever they are, the customer sees the total before confirming.

Road distance, not a straight line

Two points three kilometres apart across the Ring Road are not a three-kilometre ride. We measure the route, so the fare matches the work.

No surge, no surprise

The price quoted on the form is the price at the door. Rain does not change it and traffic does not change it.

Riders keep 88% of the fare

One flat commission of 12% a ride, taken in the app. No daily fee, no joining cost, and nothing owed on a day you do not ride.

Questions

The things vendors ask first

Still stuck on something? Call or email — a person answers, usually the same day.

Does my customer need to install anything?

No. Your link opens in whatever browser is already on their phone. They fill in three short steps and get a tracking link by SMS. Nothing to download and no account to create.

What if the customer cannot describe their address?

They do not have to. The form offers Use my location when they are standing at the place, and a map to drag a pin on when they are not. Either gives the rider a precise coordinate, which beats a street name anyway.

How do I get paid, and how does the rider?

Customers pay by Mobile Money or card through Paystack, or in cash at the door — you choose what you accept. Riders are paid the fare less a flat 12% commission per ride, settled in the app — there is no daily fee and nothing to pay on a day they do not ride.

What stops a rider marking a parcel delivered when it is not?

The four-digit PIN. It is generated for each delivery and shown only to the customer. The rider has to enter it and the system checks it before the delivery closes. Repeated wrong guesses are rate-limited.

Can I send a delivery for later?

Yes. Set a pickup time when the order is created and it waits in the queue until then, rather than going out for a rider straight away.

My area is not on the coverage list. Can you still deliver?

Often, yes — that list is where riders are concentrated, not a fence. Call us with the route and we will tell you honestly whether we can cover it today.

About SuperDelivery

SuperDelivery is operated by Anknovate IT Services, connecting vendors, dispatchers, and independent riders in one system - from a vendor's shareable ordering link, through live GPS tracking and Mobile Money payments, to PIN-verified proof of delivery at the door.

Everything a delivery business needs, in one place

No fees, no vendor lock-in — self-host it or point it at your own Supabase project.

🔗

One-tap vendor sign-up

A vendor registers once and gets a shareable link that turns into a full delivery request form for their customers — no account needed on either side.

📍

Live order tracking

Every customer gets their own private tracking link, and every dispatcher gets a live map of every rider currently on the road.

⚡

Smart zone pricing

Fares are priced automatically from real road distance, with per-zone rates and instant same-zone rider assignment when someone's available.

🗓️

Schedule ahead

Customers can pick a future date and time instead of "now" — dispatchers get an animated reminder as the scheduled time gets close, so nothing slips through.

💳

Payments & commission

Track what customers owe and what riders owe the business, side by side, with a full CSV export for your own books.

📊

Reporting built in

An always-current overview plus date-range reports across deliveries, payments, staff, vendors, and zones — no spreadsheet gymnastics required.

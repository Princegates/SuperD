Delete your account or your data

You can ask us to delete your SuperDelivery account, or just the personal details we hold, at any time. You do not need the app installed to ask, and there is no charge.

How to ask

Send us a message from the email address or phone number on your account, saying whether you want your whole account deleted or only your personal details removed. We reply to confirm before anything is deleted.

Emailinfo@anknovate.com Phone+233 55 355 2693

We aim to complete a request within 30 days of confirming who you are.

What gets deleted

If you are a customer

Your name, phone number and email address are erased from our records and from the delivery entries they appear on. Deliveries stay on file as anonymous entries, because they are the business's own record of work done and money taken.

If you are a rider

Your account is closed and you can no longer sign in. We erase your name, phone number, email address, home address, date of birth, ID and licence numbers, vehicle details and your recorded locations.

If you are a vendor

Your business is deactivated, your ordering link stops working, and the contact details we hold for you are erased.

What we keep, and why

Some records cannot be deleted on request, because we are required to keep them or need them to run the business honestly:

  • Payment and commission records — kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, so our books stay complete.
  • Delivery records — kept, but with the personal details stripped out, so they no longer identify anyone.
  • Records involved in a dispute or investigation — kept until it is resolved.
Deleting your account does not cancel money you already owe or are owed. If there is an unsettled balance either way, we will tell you when you ask, and settle it before closing the account.

Changed your mind?

Tell us before we carry out the request and we will stop. Once it is done it cannot be undone — deleted data is gone, and a closed account has to be created again from scratch.

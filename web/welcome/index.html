Three tear-off stubs, three people, no back and forth

A delivery on SuperDelivery is one docket that passes through three hands. Everyone sees the same one, so nobody has to call to ask where it got to.

01

WB-06 · The fare

Worked out the same way every time

A base fare, plus a rate for each kilometre the rider actually rides. Both are set per zone by the vendor, and the customer sees the total before confirming.

EXAMPLE · OSU → EAST LEGON

Base fareGH₵5.00
Road distance6.4 km
Rate per kmGH₵1.50
Distance chargeGH₵9.60
TotalGH₵14.60

Figures shown are the platform defaults. A vendor’s zone can carry its own base fare and per-kilometre rate, and whatever it is, the customer sees the total before they confirm.

Road distance, not a straight line

Two points 3 km apart across the Ring Road are not a 3 km ride. We measure the route, so the fare matches the work.

No surge, no surprise

The price quoted at the form is the price at the door. Rain does not change it and traffic does not change it.

Riders pay a day rate

Instead of a cut of every fare, riders settle one daily fee by Mobile Money. Busy days stay theirs.

WB-07 · Questions

The things vendors ask first

Still stuck on something? Call or email — a person answers, and usually the same day.

Does my customer need to install anything?

No. Your link opens in whatever browser is already on their phone. They fill in three short steps and get a tracking link by SMS. Nothing to download and no account to create.

What if the customer cannot describe their address?

They do not have to. The form offers Use my location when they are standing at the place, and a map they can drag a pin on when they are not. Either one gives the rider a precise coordinate, which is more useful than a street name anyway.

How do I get paid, and how does the rider?

Customers pay by Mobile Money or card through Paystack, or in cash at the door — you choose which you accept. Riders keep the fare and settle a flat daily fee in the app, so a busy day is a good day for them.

What stops a rider from marking a parcel delivered when it is not?

The four-digit PIN. It is generated for each delivery and shown only to the customer. The rider has to enter it, and the system checks it before the delivery closes. Repeated wrong guesses are rate-limited.

Can I send a delivery for later?

Yes. Set a pickup time when the order is created and it sits in the queue until then, rather than going out for a rider straight away.

My area is not in the coverage list. Can you still deliver?

Often, yes — the zone list is where riders are concentrated, not a fence. Call us with the route and we will tell you honestly whether we can cover it today.
